Frequently Asked Questions

Simple Answers.

Can you guarantee a #1 Google Maps ranking?

No. No legitimate consultant can guarantee a specific Google ranking because Google's systems continuously evaluate many signals. We focus on improving the quality and relevance of your digital presence.

Do you work with businesses outside Bengaluru?

Yes. SPDigitalJourney supports businesses across India, with Bengaluru as the base location.

Can you help with an existing Google Business Profile?

Yes. Existing profiles can be reviewed for business information, categories, services, content, reviews, local relevance and customer conversion opportunities.

What is AI Search Readiness?

It means structuring your business information and digital content so that it is clearer and more useful for modern question-based and AI-assisted discovery.
